📊 Excel Project: Income & Expense Tracker Dashboard – 2025
I'm excited to share my latest Microsoft Excel Dashboard project!

🎯 Project Objective:
To analyze income and expenses, monitor financial performance, identify spending patterns and support better financial decision-making.

🛠️ Tools & Excel Skills Used
•	Microsoft Excel
•	Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
•	Advanced Excel formulas
•	Interactive Slicers
•	KPI Cards
•	Data Visualization
•	Dashboard Design

📈 Key Insights from the Dashboard:
•	Freelancing and salary are the two largest income sources.
•	Rent is the highest expense category.
•	Shopping and bills are other major spending areas.
•	The top three expense categories account for approximately 59.5% of expenses.
•	The dashboard helps identify opportunities for better expense management.

📊Business recommendations:
"I recommend monitoring monthly expenses, setting category-wise budgets, reviewing discretionary spending and tracking monthly savings performance."
